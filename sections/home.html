Short Bio

I am a research-track professor at the School of Cryptologic Science and Engineering, Shandong University. My research interests encompass artificial intelligence security, particularly the design of provably secure AI protection systems integrated with cryptography, as well as the development of lightweight cryptographic algorithms. I have published papers at top-tier security conferences, including IEEE S&P, ACM CCS, NDSS, and USENIX Security. Meanwhile, I have been invited to serve on the program committees for USENIX Security'26, RAID'25, EuroS&P'25, ACSAC'24, PETS'25/'26, and SaTML'25, and as an area chair for ICLR'26. Notably, I lead a curated reading list on safety, security, and privacy of large models: Awesome-LM-SSP ().

I am always looking for self-motivated students to join my group, including postdocs, visiting PhDs, masters, engineers, interns (remote), and predocs. Please send me an email if you are interested.
